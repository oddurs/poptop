Findings from an adversarial review of `web/`. Thirteen charges, seven upheld,
three partly, three dismissed.

The theme running through the upheld ones: the site states principles in module
headers and then does not quite meet them, and nothing outside a laptop checks.
The fix is the same in every case — make the guarantee mechanical, or stop
claiming it.
