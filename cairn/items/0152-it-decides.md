The last step of the ladder. v5.0 explains what happened; this turns the
explanation into something a person can act on without doing arithmetic under
pressure.

Three questions get asked in every incident and no monitor answers any of them:
what would I get back if I stopped this, what happens if I do nothing, and — the
one everybody actually asks — **what is wrong with this box?**

The discipline is what keeps this from being astrology. Every answer here is a
projection from data poptop holds, it carries its own error, and it refuses
rather than guesses when the data will not support it. A monitor that is
confidently wrong about the future is worse than one that says nothing, because
somebody will act on it.
