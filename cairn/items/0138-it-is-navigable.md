Six hundred samples are retained and the only ways through them are one arrow
key at a time, a jump to a timestamp, and a report. That is a buffer you can
address but not *search*.

The filter language already answers "which processes match this" at one moment.
The same language over the time axis answers "when did this happen", which is a
question no other monitor in this category can even accept — they have no
history to search.
