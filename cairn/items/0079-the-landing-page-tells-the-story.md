The landing page works and is honest, but it lists features where it should
argue. Nine sections that carry a narrative — you always arrive too late; here
is how you go back; here is the proof; here is where we lose — each one built
around a picture that makes its own case.

The visual vocabulary is the tool's own: braille plots, the process table,
hairline rules, the reversed cursor, and measured numbers. Nothing on this page
should look like it came from a marketing kit.
